#### *Some LANs are having their last hurrah of 2026\. Some are just starting their story.*

<img width="1200" height="630" alt="November + December 2026 LANs" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b1047417-4827-4165-aa73-e45946bba6e0" />

The year is wrapping up, and multiple LANs across the globe are finding time to celebrate. Whether it’s a final event of the year or the very first, each one has something special going on to bring the community together to make memories\!

## **November**

### **Big Dapple 11: Halloween Afters \- November 7th, 2026 (New York, United States)**

New York’s Big Dapple has another costume party, this time for Halloween\! Dress to impress while trick-or-treating, raiding the Spirhalite Islands, Salmon Running, or taking part in the 4v4 tournament\! Be prepared for one of the biggest LAN locals, attended by some of the best players in North America. 

Switch 2s are permitted for this event, but those using them must bring their docks and adapters, and be willing to allow others to use them if needed. Competitors also need their controller, game, and 3.5mm headset. Attendees wearing costumes must not bring any props resembling weapons.

The main Splatoon 3 tournament will be streamed on the [GenGame Twitch Channel](https://www.twitch.tv/gengame). TOs also plan on hosting an extra Salmon Run side event, and all attendees can play Splatoon 3 or Splatoon Raiders at open stations.

Big Dapple 11: Halloween Afters takes place at 11 AM ET on Saturday, November 7th, 2026 at OS NYC in New York, New York. A detailed list of routes and garages can be found on Big Dapple’s [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/big-dapple-11/details) page, including a community-curated list of cool things to do outside of the LAN\!

### **GatorLAN Fall 2026 \- November 7th, 2026 (Florida, United States)**

GatorLAN, Southeast US’s largest free gaming event, features two days of gaming across different platforms. Taking place twice per year in the fall and spring, expect to see panels, contests, board games, crafts, and more as part of the University of Florida’s 50+ tournament LAN. In addition to being completely free, it is also open to everyone, not just university students\!

The venue is located inside the University of Florida in Gainesville, Florida. The event requires everyone to bring their own setups, which includes consoles, controllers, computers, games, etc., so if you plan to participate, pack accordingly\! Headsets are also highly recommended.

It is encouraged to bring extra setups for others to use in case of an emergency; if you are able to, there is a form to fill out on GatorLAN’s [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/gatorlan-fall-2026/details) page. Bringing an extra setup for others to use will give you an additional entry into any giveaways as a bonus\!

GatorLAN’s fall edition will run Saturday and Sunday, November 7–8th beginning at 11 AM. All events, unless otherwise specified on start.gg, will be one-day only. Splatoon 3 is a Saturday-only event, found in the Rion Ballroom.

### **Splat City Limits \- November 14th, 2026 (Texas, United States)**

Texas Splatoon and Longhorn Splatoon are coming together to bring a brand new LAN to the capital of Texas, Austin\! Dubbed Splat City Limits, the event offers not just a tournament, but free spectating, a community meetup, merchandise, and much more\! 

<img width="560" height="700" alt="SCL" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b11cb90-4d9a-4dd9-90b2-dce14e374f3f" />

*One of the many Splat City Limits advertising posters from Texas Splatoon. Featured in the center is SCL’s mascot, Bandit.*

The 4v4 Pools-into-Bracket tournament will be played with a predetermined maplist. Switch 1s, Switch 2s, and even Switch Lites are accommodated, but players will need to bring their own console along with their game, controller, and 3.5mm headset.

Splat City Limits will have their inaugural LAN on Saturday, November 14th: TOs highly recommend showing up ahead of the 12:30 PM start time so you can find parking and get checked in. The event has a [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/splat-city-limits/details) page that lists rules, directions to the Alienware Longhorn Esports Lounge & Arena, and has a link to the LAN’s Discord.

### **Chi-Shoals \#46 \- November 21st, 2026 (Illinois, United States)**

Illinois’ established Splatoon LAN, Chi-Shoals, will be at Chicago’s Harold Washington Library for one last event in 2026\. The next Chi-Shoals will be in 2027\! As always, anyone can attend for free, regardless of age or skill level.

The main tournament will be a 4v4 Groups to Single-Elimination format. To take part, please bring a Nintendo Switch 1 (no Switch 2s), copy of Splatoon 3, 3.5mm wired headset, and a controller. And most importantly, yourself\! For those who can’t attend, the tournament will be streamed on the [SquidWest Twitch Channel](https://twitch.tv/squidwest).

Chi-Shoals \#46 will open up at 11 AM CT on Saturday, November 21st, and will run until 4 PM. You can find more information on SquidWest’s [Challonge](https://challonge.com/communities/SquidWest) page, or you can join the [SquidWest (Midwest Splatoon Players) Discord](https://discord.com/invite/Acv9qH6) server for further info and commentator opportunities\!

### **LAN of 10,000 Lakes \#5: Giving Season \- November 21st, 2026 (Minnesota, United States)**

LAN of 10,000 Lakes is opening their arms and making this event extra special\! Plenty of new events and goodies await in the Twin Cities area of Minnesota\! You’ll find this LAN on the third floor of a wheelchair-accessible office building, inside the RTM Gaming Lounge.

Participants of the main tournament need to bring their own Switch and game, controller, and headset in order to play. Switch 2 owners can participate, but will need to bring their own dock and LAN cables. Those who bring a Switch 1 dock and AC adapter and are willing to share with others will get \$2 off their registration fee.

Last time, LAN of 10,000 Lakes offered a Tableturf side event. This time, you’ll find a Splatoon Raiders speedrunning event, starting at 10:30 AM CT. Players won’t need to have their own Switch 2 or copy of the game to partake—setups will be provided, and the speedrunning finals will be streamed at [LAN of 10,000 Lakes’ Twitch](http://twitch.tv/lanof10000lakes). Anyone registered for the main tournament is eligible for the side event.

…But wait, there’s more\! There will also be giveaways before every round of the main tournament’s Swiss stage\! Prizes include amiibos, Splatoon soundtrack CDs, and Nintendo eShop cards. Anyone competing in the Splatoon 3 main event is eligible for the giveaways.

LAN of 10,000 Lakes \#5 unleashes their gift-giving reckoning on Saturday, November 21st, at the RunTheMix (RTM) Gaming Lounge in Roseville, Minnesota. RTM’s doors open at 10 AM CT, the speedrunning side event starts at 10:30 AM, and the Swiss bracket starts at 1 PM. [Start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/lan-of-10-000-lakes-5-splatoon-3-lan/details) has venue directions, tournament rules, Discord server invitation, and registration.

### **SeaBATTLE\! 11 — Wishing Hour \- November 21st, 2026 (Washington, United States)**

Luck is on everyone’s side for SeaBATTLE\! 11 — Wishing Hour\! The eleventh iteration of Seattle’s local returns to GameWorks, located downtown with nearby public transport and an in-house kitchen to order food and drinks. While nearly a month after Halloween, wearing costumes is encouraged\!

Tournament participants need their Switch, controller, Splatoon 3, and 3.5mm headset. Switch 2s are allowed, but users will need their own Switch 2 dock and adapter, labeled, and need to select the appropriate console option when registering on [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/seabattle-11-wishing-hour/details). Tournament winners will receive part of the prize pool, medals, and a badge for their Sendou.ink profile\!

SeaBATTLE\! 11 — Wishing Hour wishes that you’ll be at the GameWorks video arcade in Seattle, Washington on Saturday, November 21st. Check-in starts at 11 AM, and Wave A Pools start at 12 PM. More event information, including a Discord invitation and schedule, can be found on SeaBATTLE’s start.gg page. SplatoonTourney will be streaming this event on Twitch\!

### **AADL Hyperlocal \- November 28th, 2026 (Michigan, United States)**

Michigan’s smaller LAN, the Ann Arbor District Library Hyperlocal, is gearing up for its last event for 2026, and for a while. While the AADL Hyperlocal may have its 2027 debut delayed, keep an eye out for announcements regarding its new sister LAN, Rumble at the Rhino\!

The tourney will run from 1 PM to 4 PM ET. The hosting venue will provide the game setups and gear, so all that attendees need to bring is themselves. Having started with the September event, the tournament is also planned to be streamed on the [AADL YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@AADL). Participants of all ages and skill levels are welcome to join in, free of charge.

The November 2026 AADL Hyperlocal will be at the Ann Arbor District Downtown Library in the 4th floor Program Room from 1–4 PM on Saturday, November 28th. Registration is not required for this event. Further details can be found on the AADL’s website: [https\://aadl.org/node/666427](https://aadl.org/node/666427).

### **Squid Social 37 \- November 29th, 2026 (Toronto, Canada)**

Squid Social, just one of Splatoon Toronto’s LANs, has their November monthly near the end of the month. Having recently moved to the University of Toronto, they are building up to the return of their yearly main event, Torontoroka\!

<img width="516" height="800" alt="SS35" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8f9e3f5a-d779-4ffb-b1d4-8278f8352e92" />

*The exclusive poster for Squid Social 35, held in September 2026\. The art was done by [@fyscuit.cafe](https://bsky.app/profile/fyscuit.cafe).*

This event is just as much for casual friendlies as it is for serious tournaments—both a draft bracket and open lobbies are held during Squid Social’s runtime. Participants should bring their Nintendo Switch, Splatoon 3, power cable, and a controller. Docks are only required if players are not playing in handheld mode. A LAN adapter is needed if not using a docked Switch 2, and a 3.5mm wired headset is recommended. 

Squid Social 37 takes over Room 120 at Woodsworth College in Toronto, Ontario, and will be open from 1–6 PM on Sunday, November 29th. Keep up with their upcoming LANs in the [Splatoon Toronto Discord](https://discord.com/invite/squidsocial) and at their website: [https\://splatoon.ca/](https://splatoon.ca/).

## **December**

### **AREA CUP Live\! \- December 5th, 2026 (Tokyo, Japan)**

The most prestigious community-run Splatoon tournament… Now in person\! AREA CUP Live\! is the first AREA CUP LAN in a long time. Interested teams must submit an application (found on [Ikanakama](https://ikanakama.ink/events/20906)); the tournament is capped at 16 teams and is first-come, first-served. Additional applicants will be put on a waitlist.

The tournament format is based on the number of teams that are signed up. If all 16 team slots are filled, the event will be Single-Elimination with no qualifier stage. Participants need to bring their Nintendo Switch 2, controller (wired connection is preferred), and copy of the game. Headsets will be provided by the venue.

AREA CUP Live\! starts at 12 PM JT (7 PM PT / 10 PM ET) on Saturday, December 5th. The venue is the eSpofield gaming and community space in Tokyo, Japan’s Itabashi Ward. A livestream on [AREA CUP’s YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@splatzonejp) channel is planned\!

### **Rocky Mountain Splatdown \#5: Blizzard Blitz \- December 5th, 2026 (Colorado, United States)**

Rocky Mountain Splatdown is starting a new chapter in Colorado’s LAN books: new venue, new equipment, new staff, and new prizes\! It has relocated to the University of Denver, with DU Esports hosting the event. Show up and get an exclusive poster\!

Participants can use a Switch 1 or 2, and will need to bring their own copy of Splatoon 3 and first-party controller with the console. TOs highly recommend bringing your own 3.5mm headset with mic; if you forget yours, you can rent a pair from the venue for \$5. Switch 2 users will also need to bring their docks and all necessary cables. If you can, bringing extra 3.5mm aux cables and Switch 1 docks and cables would help the TOs\!

Rocky Mountain Splatdown \#5: Blizzard Blitz begins their new era on Saturday, December 5th at the University of Denver’s Centennial Halls. Doors open at 10:30 AM, and the tournament will begin at 11 AM. Tournament rules, guidelines, Discord link, and registration can be found on the [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/rocky-mountain-splatdown-5-blizzard-blitz/details) page\!

### **CALterna 09 \- 2 Year Anniversary \- December 5th, 2026 (California, United States)**

The two-year anniversary of CALterna is imminent\! At B.Y.O. Games in Los Angeles, California, this will be the last CALterna event for a while, so be there to celebrate the special occasion\!

For the 4v4 main event, players need their Switch 1 or Switch 2\. Switch 1 users need to bring their Switch 1 docks; Switch 2 users do not. A controller and copy of Splatoon 3 are also needed, and a 3.5mm headset is recommended. The 4v4 event is capped at 12 teams; you can register on [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/calterna-09-2-year-anniversary/details).

CALterna 09 celebrates two years of running on Saturday, December 5th, and the doors to B.Y.O. Games open at 12:30 PM PT. Can’t make it? Watch from home on [Twitch](https://twitch.tv/pickpickles)\!

### **Sunset Surge: Twilight Masquerade \- December 12th, 2026 (Arizona, United States)**

Arizona may be cooling down, but Sunset Surge remains as hot as ever\! Recently celebrating hitting a double-digit number of events, this 3+ years-running LAN has some new and exciting changes for its latest event. The masquerade theme isn’t just for intrigue—go all out and dress to the nines\!

The main event is capped at 16 teams and will be played on all four ranked modes. Players should bring their Switch (1s and 2s allowed), dock and cables (regardless of Switch version), controller, game, and 3.5mm headset. Handheld players will be accommodated; reach out to staff in the [Sunset Surge \- Arizona Splatoon Discord](https://discord.com/invite/xmcsyFPrKN) server so the team can bring the necessary equipment.

Want to lend a hand? Take a look at their [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/sunset-surge-3-year-anniversary-arizona-splatoon-lan/details) page for commentator, volunteer, and art contest information\! You’ll also find map directions, tournament rules, a treasure trove of art and photos from past events, and more reasons to attend\!

Or check out the Sunset Surge team’s [Bluesky video](https://bsky.app/profile/sunsetsurge.ink/post/3n2337jl5i22s), posted during the countdown of each day until the masquerade begins.

Sunset Surge: Twilight Masquerade will be ready to welcome you on Saturday, December 12th. The tournament will be streamed on [SplatoonTourney’s Twitch Channel](https://www.twitch.tv/splatoontourney). You can find Sunset Surge at the ASU Esports Arena on the 3rd Floor, Room \#315, in Tempe, Arizona—the fifth floor will also be open for hanging out and eating\!

### **SQUID STORM XVIII \- December 12th, 2026 (Fellbach, Germany)**

Germany’s bimonthly LAN, SQUIDSTORM, is hosted at the CLUTCH23 Gaming Bar & Social Space. Players can feast and fight in one place—as a bonus, a day pass comes with one free drink\! Doors open at 11 AM, and the bar opens at 3 PM.

Two 4v4 matches can run concurrently thanks to the venue’s 16-monitor stations. The tournament format is a Best of 3 Swiss into Playoffs bracket. Players need to bring their Nintendo Switch, controller, Splatoon 3, and a dock with LAN adapter and power cable. 

SQUIDSTORM XVIII runs on Saturday, December 12th. The bracket is planned to start at 12 PM, one hour after doors open. You will find all of the details, registration, and rules on [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/squid-storm-18-by-clutch-united/details). Can’t make it out? [CLUTCH United’s Twitch](https://twitch.tv/clutchunited) channel will be streaming the event\!

### **Music City of Color \- December 13th, 2026 (Tennessee, United States)**

Tennessee’s first Splatoon LAN local has been in progress for a long time, and they’re thrilled that Music City of Color is nearly here\! Located in Smyrna at the very center of Tennessee, you’ll find yourself in the Nashville metro area with plenty to see, hear, and participate in while you’re there.

The LAN has its own [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/music-city-of-color-2/details) page, which include links to MCoC’s Bluesky, Discord, YouTube, and email. A maplist has already been posted. Venue directions and registration is also available, and the page will list event information regarding the tournament, required equipment, and any other details for attendees.

Music City of Color, Tennessee’s premier Splatoon event, kicks off on Sunday, December 13th, bright and early at 10 AM CT\! Registration closes right before the LAN starts, so there is ample time to get signed up. Exclusive posters will be up for grabs at the venue: the Smyrna Assembly Hall.

### **SplashGo\! \#14.5 \- December 27th, 2026 (Osaka, Japan)**

The second major Japanese LAN of December, SplashGo\!, caps off the list of 2026 LANs. SplashGo\! is Japan’s Turf War-only tournament series, and prior to Riptide 2026, was the world’s largest Splatoon LAN. SplashGo\! \#14.5 has a 48-team limit, so it won’t be taking back that title, but will still be home to some of the fiercest Splatoon competition around. 

Those who are competing will need to bring their Nintendo Switch 2, dock, power cables, controller, and game. A wired controller connection is preferred, so plan to bring the charging cable. The Preliminary Round will have eight blocks of Round Robin, with up to six teams per block if all 48 team slots are filled. The Final Round will be Single-Elimination.

Looking for commemorative goodies? Participants will get player badges and stickers based on their placement, and all attendees will be given a pamphlet with team and event info. A merchandise table will also be onsite. Before the finals, the venue’s lights will go dark, and as players enter, it is encouraged for everyone to cheer and wave around glowsticks, which will be distributed to players and spectators\!

SplashGo\! \#14.5 will be held on the second floor of MyDome Osaka, located in the Chuo Ward of Osaka, Japan, on Sunday, December 27th. Registration and further inquiries can be found on the event’s [start.gg](https://www.start.gg/tournament/splashgo-14-5-in-osaka/details) page or official website, [Ikanakama.ink](https://ikanakama.ink/events/22493).

As 2026 comes to a close, we can turn our heads to the future that 2027 will bring. More brand-new LANs are in store, both of the announced and unannounced variety, and many more LANs will make it to their first anniversaries. The first quarter of the new year also brings with it North America’s LAN Championship Showdown season, so the excitement and action won’t be slowing down one bit\!
