Teams post when they are looking for a scrim (a practice match), other teams request to play and the poster accepts one. The accepted pairing gets a match page with a chat and map-by-map tracking.

A **post** (`ScrimPost`) is made by a **side**: a team or a pick-up of users. Another side makes a **request** (`ScrimPostRequest`) to it and accepting one books the scrim, the post's side playing as `ALPHA` and the request's as `BRAVO`. The rules are listed under Behaviour below, each with the tests that establish it.

## Lifecycle

<Mermaid code={`stateDiagram-v2
	[*] --> Open: posted on /scrims/new
	Open --> Open: requests sent and withdrawn
	Open --> [*]: deleted, or another scrim of its players booked
	Open --> Booked: a request accepted
	Booked --> Canceled: canceled before the start
	Booked --> Locked: tracking idle long enough after the start
	Canceled --> [*]
	Locked --> [*]`} />

`/scrims` lists posts until 3 hours past their start, booked ones only to their players. A post nobody accepted is never deleted, it just stops being listed.

## Map-by-map

<Mermaid code={`flowchart LR
	submit["A side submits its map list"] --> pool["Pool: union of the submitted lists"]
	pool --> next["Next map generated, avoiding played ones"]
	next --> report["Winner reported"]
	report --> next
	next -. "pick or replay a map" .-> next
	report -. "undo the latest report" .-> next`} />

Every change is broadcast through the scrim's chat room so both sides' pages update.
