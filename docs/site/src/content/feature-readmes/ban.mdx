A site ban locks a user out of sendou.ink: whatever page they open they are sent to `/suspended`, which shows the reason they were given and when the ban ends. Staff ban and unban from the admin page.

This is the ban from the whole site. A tournament organization banning a player from its own tournaments is a separate thing, part of the `tournament-organization` feature.

## Vocabulary

- **Ban** (`User.banned`): `0` (or `null`) for none, `1` for a ban without an end, otherwise the timestamp the ban ends at. Nothing runs when a timed ban ends, the column keeps its value and the ban just stops holding from then on (`BanStatus.isActive`). `User.bannedReason` is shown to the banned user.
- **Ban log** (`BanLog`): every ban and unban made by staff, with who made it, shown on the user's admin page (`/u/:identifier/admin`).
- **Automatic ban**: one the site lays down itself, without anyone in the ban log. The only one so far is submitting a friend code another account already has, which usually means a second account and is sorted out on the Discord helpdesk.

The rules (who is sent where, what gets logged) are listed under Behaviour below, each with the tests that establish it.

## What a ban blocks

Banning also deletes the user's API tokens. Being sent to `/suspended` is checked on every request by the root middleware (`auth/core/user-context.server.ts`), the other places check `Bans.isBanned` themselves:

- the public API rejects a banned user's tokens
- a banned player can't register for a tournament or be added to a team (`SITE_BANNED` of `tournament/core/TeamEligibility.server.ts`), be part of a scrim pick-up or join a voice call
- banned users are left out of plus server tier assignment, the SendouQ group invite list of friends and the sidebar's X Rank streams

## Where to start reading

`core/Bans.server.ts` keeps who is banned in memory, so the check on every request costs no query, and everything writing `User.banned` refreshes it: the `BAN_USER`, `UNBAN_USER` and `MIGRATE` actions of `admin/actions/admin.server.ts` and the automatic ban of the `ADD_FRIEND_CODE` action in `sendouq/actions/q.server.ts`. `core/BanStatus.ts` reads a `User.banned` value. The writes themselves are `banUser` and `unbanUser` of `admin/AdminRepository.server.ts`, the admin actions also announcing them on Discord through a webhook.
