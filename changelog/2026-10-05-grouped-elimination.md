Elimination brackets in groups and unplayed rounds

- Single and double elimination brackets can be split into groups, each group playing its own bracket
- Choose which of the last rounds are played, e.g. no grand finals or no bracket reset. Teams still in the bracket when it ends share their placement
- Round skipping available for both grouped and standard variants (many 1st placers is possible e.g. for qualifiers)
- Matches in the bracket show where the winner and loser continue to
