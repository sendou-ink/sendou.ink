Lobbies for playing 8s

- Make a lobby for right now or later, others sign up and check in
- Teams are reshuffled after every set to keep them even and mix up who plays with whom
- Up to 2 extra players spectate and rotate in, everyone else joins the waitlist
- Team and lobby chats with voice that follows you to your next team
- A leaderboard of sets, maps and weapons played
