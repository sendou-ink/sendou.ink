Custom theme improvements

- Yellow and other light accent colors are now vivid instead of muddy
- In light mode buttons, toggles, checkboxes and badges with a light accent or secondary color are bright with dark text
- New slider to make the dark mode background darker
- Accent chroma slider goes higher so every hue can be as vivid as the screen allows
- Accent colored text on tinted backgrounds (chips, tags) is easier to read in light mode
