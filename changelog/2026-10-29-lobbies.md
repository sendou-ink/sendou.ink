Lobbies (8s) can now be created by Supporters and played by anyone

- Host a lobby for right now or schedule it for later, others sign up and check in before it starts
- Best-of sets played 4v4 with the teams reshuffled after every set
- Extra players wait on the waitlist or spectate and rotate in when a spot opens up
- Each lobby has its own leaderboard, separate from SendouQ
- Keep a lobby public, share it only via an invite link or limit it to your associations
