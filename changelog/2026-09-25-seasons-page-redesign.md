Seasons page redesign

- Season picker shows your rank for every season at a glance
- Activity calendar from image export shows the days you played SendouQ, tournaments or both
- Team rank and a quick stats preview (best stage, most played weapon & mode, top teammate) on the season page
- Season history is grouped by day with the day's SP change, and can be filtered to only SendouQ or only tournaments
- Stats now have their own dedicated page (largely the same as before)
- New line graph component previously used in image export
- Win rates by stage & mode shown as a heatmap presentation
- Teammates and opponents lists show when you played them over the course of a season
