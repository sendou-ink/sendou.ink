Menus, dialogs and forms are faster and lighter across the site

- Snappier performance with different UI elements
- The site's menus, side bar, mobile tab bar and dialogs open right away, without waiting for the page's scripts to load
- Tournament pages show their content right away instead of a loading placeholder while the page's scripts load
- Date and time fields now use your browser's own picker
- Select, date and time fields show their error message when the value is invalid, including a clearer message for a half-filled time range
- Dropdowns & popovers close when scrolled under the top bar instead of floating over it
- Measures against ads covering the bottom navigation on mobile
