Language changes

- Weapon, gear, stage and mode names can be shown in a different language than the rest of the site. Pick the one your game is in from the Locale settings
- In-game names are available in every language Splatoon 3 supports
- The site itself is now available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Chinese. Danish, Hebrew, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Italian, Korean, Russian and Canadian French translations were removed
- Spanish and French speakers are shown the variant of their region by default
