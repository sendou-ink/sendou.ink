Comp analyzer range chart shows more than max range

- Toggle other ways of shooting a weapon: roller and Splatana swings, Squeezer hold, Ballpoint short range, S-BLAST jump shots, charger taps, stringer partial charges and dualie turrets
- Rollers and brushes mark how far they still splat in their fewest hits
- Show the ranges of the comp's special weapons
- Hide a weapon from the range chart by clicking its icon
- Wider page and a taller range chart, so overlapping trajectories are easier to tell apart
