Broadcast overlays are now available for every tournament

- Organizers and streamers run their stream from the tournament's Broadcast tab, OBS shows whatever is on air
- Rosters, map list, the game's scoreboard, results, the bracket and the hold screens are all filled in from the tournament automatically
- Make the overlays your own with a theme, the Broadcast overlays guide on docs.sendou.ink shows how
