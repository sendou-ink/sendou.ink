More pages now update live without needing a refresh

- The teams checked in count on the bracket page updates as teams check in
- The bracket page updates when the organizer resets a bracket, changes seeds or progression, removes or drops a team, finalizes the tournament or locks a match to be casted
- Players who join or get added to a team leave a tournament's LFG page right away
- Group members on the SendouQ preparing page follow along when someone joins the queue or leaves the group
- Groups that time out drop off the SendouQ looking page right away
- The other team's scrim page shows a canceled scrim as canceled
- Reported weapons and scanned scoreboards show up for everyone on SendouQ, tournament match and lobby pages
