Scanner coach mode: VoD reviewing taken to the next level

- Alternative way to view a VoD fed to the Scanner
- Key moments are listed by type, each one jumps the video to it
- Moments covered: special uses, openings, pushes, holds, retakes, special stacks, deaths with special, death streaks, kill streaks, staggers and team wipes
- Filter the games by match type, mode, stage, knockout and either team's weapons or players. Playback skips the games filtered out
- Quick controls to step between games, lives, moments or 5 seconds at a time, change the playback speed and swap the map with the video, each with its own hotkey
- Timeline of the game being played under the video showing players' status and the objective, click anywhere on it to skip there
- Works on cast VoDs too, coached from the team you pick
- Saves minimap frames you can draw on
- For more extensive planning, take the minimap frame + weapons to the full Map Planner
