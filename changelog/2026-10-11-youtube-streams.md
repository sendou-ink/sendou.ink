YouTube live streams (beta)

- YouTube streams now show on tournament and SendouQ streams pages, the sidebar and user pages, next to Twitch streams. Add #sendouink to your stream's title or description to get it picked up
- Tournament organizers can add YouTube channels as cast accounts
- Matches played on a YouTube stream get their VoDs linked automatically, same as Twitch
- Streaming on both Twitch and YouTube at the same time shows as one stream with a link to each
- Redesigned stream cards: platform links and viewer count over the thumbnail, click the streamer to see their user card and the team name to go to the team page
- "Stream not showing up?" help on the streams pages explains how to get your stream shown
