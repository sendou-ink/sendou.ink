Broadcast overlays show what's playing on the hold and casters scenes

- Drop music files into the dashboard's Music tab and play them on stream, straight from your browser to OBS without uploading them
- The track playing shows on a now playing line with its title and artist, both editable
- Play, pause, skip, shuffle and set the volume from the dashboard, the music fades out on the other scenes
- Playing music on your own computer instead? Type what's playing and it shows on the same line
