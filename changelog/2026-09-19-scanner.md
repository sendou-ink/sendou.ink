Scanner is out for everyone

- Find it in the Tools menu. Start a capture and leave it running while you play: every game is read off your capture card and the results upload to sendou.ink as each game ends
- Your best moments are clipped automatically: a streak of four splats saves a clip with audio, and the 20 best are kept across sessions until you download them
- Scan a recorded VoD the same way, drop the file on the page and the games, clips and upload state show up as it runs
- Each game's card shows the scoreboard, the objective and player-status timeline, your deaths and kills with the matching clip a click away, and the builds read for both teams
- Your play is grouped into sessions you can come back to for 30 days, and every session or file exports as CSV
- Logging in is only needed for uploading; capturing, scanning and clips work without an account
