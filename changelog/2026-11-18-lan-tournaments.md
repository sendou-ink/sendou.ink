Running in-person (LAN) tournaments on sendou.ink

- Tournaments can be marked as in-person events with a venue picked by searching its address
- Times of a LAN are entered and shown in the venue's local time, with your own time underneath when it differs
- LANs show their venue with an "Open in maps" link, and calendar cards show where they are held
- New LAN map on the calendar showing upcoming in-person events around the world
- New pool captain staff role that can report and undo scores
- Organizers can add players without a sendou.ink account to LAN teams. Their results can be moved to their account once they make one
- Organizers can add stations (the setups at the venue), and matches are assigned to free stations automatically in play order
- New stations board where organizers can move matches between stations by dragging them
- Players see their match's station and an estimate of when it starts on the match page, bracket and header status
- Teams get a notification when it is their turn to play on a station, and when their match moves to another station
- New public display screen for the venue showing what is played now and next on every station
- Players can step through the upcoming maps of their match on the match banner
