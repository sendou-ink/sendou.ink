Ongoing activities in the header

- SendouQ and tournament status, voice chat and the scanner each get their own button in the header, opening a panel with the details
- Voice chat can be joined, muted and deafened from any page, the button shows when you are muted or deafened
- Voice chat controls moved out of the chat sidebar into the header
- Once you have used the scanner, a capture can be started and stopped from any page, with a live preview of what is being captured
