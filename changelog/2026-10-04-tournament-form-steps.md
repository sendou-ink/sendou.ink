New tournament creation form

- The form is split into steps: basics, teams & rules, maps, format and prizes & review
- Build the bracket progression visually: drag brackets into columns and connect them to send teams from one bracket to the next
- Pick who moves on from a list like "Lost in the semifinals" instead of typing placements
- See a summary of the tournament in the review step before creating it
- Works better on mobile
