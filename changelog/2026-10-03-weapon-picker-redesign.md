New way to pick a weapon on the builds, build analyzer and object damage calculator pages

- On phones, pick a category first and then the weapon from that category
- On wider screens, categories sit in a sidebar next to the weapons, with weapons of the same kit side by side
- Search weapons by name
- See each weapon's sub and special at a glance, plus its build count on the builds page
- Quick links to the weapons in your match profile
- Subs and specials have categories of their own in the object damage calculator
- The page of a picked weapon has a back link to its category
