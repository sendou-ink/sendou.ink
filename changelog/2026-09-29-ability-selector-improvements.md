Ability selector improvements in the build analyzer and build form

- Drag and drop now works more consistently across platforms
- Drag placed abilities to another slot to move or swap them, or drag them away to remove them
- Abilities that have no free slot left look disabled
- Hovering an ability shows a faint preview of it in the slot it would go into
- Abilities are grouped by where they can be used
