Weapon ability stats & popular builds redesign

- Popular builds show their most common gear layout, how many players run them and link to the analyzer and builds like them
- See which abilities the popular builds share and how varied the weapon's builds are
- Ability stats show how often each ability is run, how much AP builds put into it and how the weapon compares to others in its class
- Primary-only abilities are grouped by the gear they go on
- Both cover builds updated in about the last year by default, pick another patch or any time to change it
- Tabs to move between a weapon's builds, ability stats and popular builds
